Why Bangladesh

Six reasons Indian students choose Bangladesh

Not brochure slogans — the things that actually shape your six years as a medical student.

A course built like India’s

Five years of study, then a 12-month rotating internship, taught from the same English textbooks used in Indian colleges. Indian students here say the curriculum closely resembles the one back home.

A strong FMGE record

In 2023, Bangladesh graduates had the highest FMGE pass rate of the major MBBS destinations, and in 2024 nearly one in three passed. What you learn here carries over to India’s exam.

Busy hospitals, real cases

Colleges run their own teaching hospitals of 390 to 750 beds, crowded with patients. You see dengue, tuberculosis, diabetes and the other illnesses you will treat in India.

The shortest trip home

A direct flight from Chennai to Dhaka takes about 2 hours 40 minutes, and the clocks run only 30 minutes ahead of India. Parents can visit for a weekend.

Degrees from public universities

Private medical colleges are affiliated to public universities such as the University of Dhaka, Chittagong Medical University and Rajshahi University, which award the MBBS degree.

Food and culture you know

Rice, fish and dal on the table, Bangla in the streets, and Durga Puja and Eid celebrated across the country. Of all the MBBS destinations, it feels most like home.

How the course works

From Class 12 to doctor: the Bangladesh route

Bangladesh offers a direct MBBS in four phases, each closing with a university professional exam, then a year of internship. There is no pre-medical degree.

  1. 1
    Phase 1 · 1.5 years

    Basic sciences

    Anatomy, physiology and biochemistry, the foundation of everything after.

  2. 2
    Phases 2–3 · 2 years

    Para-clinical

    Pharmacology, forensic medicine, pathology, microbiology and community medicine, with ward visits starting.

  3. 3
    Phase 4 · 1.5 years

    Clinical

    Medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology and their branches, on the wards of the college hospital.

  4. 4
    Year 6 · 12 months

    Internship

    Rotating, supervised internship in the college’s own hospital, as NMC requires.

    We confirm this before you apply
  5. 5
    Back in India

    FMGE / NExT

    Clear the licensing exam, complete any internship NMC asks of you, and register to practise.

In all, plan for six years in Bangladesh, then the licensing exam in India. Your counsellor will confirm the exact plan for your college.

NMC status · as of Oct 2026

What’s confirmed — and what we check for you

Parents have heard a lot about Bangladesh since 2024. Here is where things actually stand for Indian students, in plain words.

Confirmed

  • The same NMC rules as everywhere: FMGL 2021 asks for at least 54 months of study in English and a 12-month internship at the same institution. Bangladesh’s five-plus-one MBBS is built this way.
  • Visas are flowing again: Bangladesh resumed all visa services for Indian nationals on 20 February 2026, after a pause that began on 22 December 2025.
  • Room for foreign students: each private medical college may fill up to 45% of its seats with foreign students, and in 2025–26 far fewer applied than there were seats.
Worth knowing

Three things we are honest about

Calm is recent. Unrest in August 2024 and again in December 2025 kept students indoors for a time; things have been calmer since the February 2026 election. Language: exams are in English, but some teachers explain in Bangla and patients speak it. If you plan to go to the US later: Bangladesh’s accreditation council is not yet WFME-recognised (June 2026). This does not change the NMC rules for practising in India.

Eligibility

Can you apply? The checklist

Every NMC point below must be true for your degree to count in India — plus Bangladesh’s own entry minimum.

  • NEET-UG qualifiedRequired for every Indian student studying medicine abroad. The result stays valid for three years.
  • 50% in Physics, Chemistry and BiologyIn Class 12, with English as a subject. 40% for SC, ST and OBC candidates.
  • Bangladesh’s own academic minimumSet each year in the DGME notice for foreign students, and usually stricter than NMC’s 50%. We match your Class 10 and 12 marks to it.
  • 17 years old by 31 DecemberOf the year you join.
  • No extra entrance testPrivate colleges admit foreign students on their Class 10 and 12 grades and NEET result.
  • A course that meets FMGL 2021At least 54 months of study, taught in English, with a 12-month internship at the same college.

FAQs

Questions parents ask us most

Straight answers. Anything else, just ask a counsellor.

Is an MBBS from Bangladesh valid in India?
Yes, if the course meets every NMC FMGL 2021 condition: at least 54 months of study, taught in English, a 12-month internship at the same institution, and eligibility for a medical licence in Bangladesh. Bangladesh’s five-year MBBS with a one-year internship in the college hospital fits this pattern. You then clear FMGE or NExT to practise in India.
How much does it cost?
An average year costs about ₹8.9 lakh all-in: roughly ₹6.8 lakh tuition, ₹1.4 lakh hostel and meals and about ₹60,000 for personal costs, visa and insurance (indicative, Oct 2026). Most private colleges quote one package for the whole course, typically US$30,000–42,000 (about ₹28–40 lakh), and collect a large part of it at admission. See the fee breakdown.
How do Bangladesh graduates do in FMGE?
According to NBEMS figures, 26.79% of Bangladesh graduates passed FMGE in 2023 — the highest among the major MBBS destinations that year — and 32.39% passed in 2024 (914 of 2,822). Results depend on your own preparation, and we guide you on FMGE coaching from the early years.
Is it taught in English?
Textbooks and examinations are in English. Some teachers explain in Bangla, and patients speak Bangla, so you pick up the language for the wards. NMC requires English-medium teaching, so we confirm it in writing for your college.
Is Bangladesh safe for Indian students now?
Indian students have continued to study in Bangladesh through recent unrest, in August 2024 and again in December 2025, when students were advised to move in groups and avoid going out late. Things have been calmer since the February 2026 election, and Bangladesh resumed visa services for Indian nationals on 20 February 2026. We share the latest advisory before you travel and stay in touch throughout the course. See NMC status above.
How long is the course?
Six years in all: five years of study in four phases, followed by a compulsory 12-month rotating internship in the college’s own hospital. See how the course works.
Do I need NEET?
Yes. NMC requires every Indian student who studies medicine abroad to qualify NEET-UG, and the result stays valid for three years. Bangladesh also sets its own academic minimum for foreign students in each year’s DGME notice.
Can I get a government college seat?
Only a few. Bangladesh’s government colleges keep a small SAARC quota; the 2025–26 notice listed 21 MBBS seats for India, applied for through the official government channel. Almost all Indian students study at private medical colleges.
How far is Bangladesh from India?
US-Bangla Airlines flies direct from Chennai to Dhaka in about 2 hours 40 minutes, five times a week from 13 November 2026. Bangladesh is just 30 minutes ahead of Indian time.
