Why Georgia

Six reasons Indian students choose Georgia

Not brochure slogans — the things that actually shape your six years as a medical student.

A European-style medical degree

Georgia is part of Europe’s Bologna Process, so the six-year MD is built on 360 ECTS credits — the same credit system used by medical schools across Europe.

English from lecture hall to ward

In English-medium programmes the whole course, clinical training included, is taught in English. That is exactly what NMC asks for — and what many countries cannot promise.

Real patients from Year 4

Clinical rotations start in the fourth year, and the whole sixth year is an internship in teaching hospitals. Indian students train in the same clinics, with the same rights, as Georgian students.

A head start on global exams

Some universities, such as David Tvildiani Medical University, build their curriculum around the US licensing exam (USMLE) — useful if you dream of a career beyond India too.

A large Indian community

An estimated 20,000–25,000 Indian students study medicine in Georgia, and India now has a resident embassy in Tbilisi. You will find seniors from your state in your first week.

Close to home, closer to Europe

About nine hours from Chennai with one stop, and only one and a half hours behind Indian time. Calls home fit easily around lectures.

How the course works

From Class 12 to doctor: the Georgian route

Georgia offers a direct six-year MD — the same level as an Indian MBBS. There is no separate pre-medical degree. Here is how the years add up.

  1. 1
    Years 1–3

    Basic medical sciences

    Anatomy, physiology, biochemistry and pathology, plus Georgian language classes so you can talk to patients later.

  2. 2
    Years 4–5

    Clinical rotations

    Clerkships in the university’s clinics and partner hospitals across medicine, surgery, paediatrics and more.

  3. 3
    Year 6 · 12 months

    Internship

    A full year of supervised practice in teaching hospitals. NMC requires it at the same university you studied in.

    We confirm this before you apply
  4. 4
    Back in India

    FMGE / NExT

    Clear the licensing exam, complete any internship NMC asks of you, and register to practise.

In all, plan for six years abroad — 360 ECTS credits — then the licensing exam in India. Your counsellor will confirm the exact plan for your university.

The 2026 changes · as of Oct 2026

Georgia changed in 2026 — here’s what it means for you

Two things moved this year: where international students can enrol, and a question about the licence Georgian graduates receive. Many websites skip both. We explain them in plain words, because they decide which university is right for you.

Confirmed

  • New students join private universities: on 5 December 2025 the Education Minister announced that state universities stop admitting new foreign students, apart from exchange and bilateral programmes. Private universities stay open, and students already enrolled are not affected.
  • The course matches NMC’s structure: Georgia’s accreditation body, NCEQE, confirmed on 14 October 2026 that the MD lasts at least six years (360 ECTS), the sixth year is a full internship, and English programmes are in English throughout.
  • Help close at hand: India now has a resident embassy and ambassador in Tbilisi.
Worth knowing

The “junior doctor” question

NMC requires that you can practise in the country where you studied, on par with its own graduates. Georgian MD graduates work as junior doctors; independent practice comes after a state exam and residency. On 24 September 2026 NMC said junior doctor status counts only if it legally allows practice at the required level. NCEQE replied on 14 October 2026 that foreign and Georgian graduates get identical rights and a registration number. The matter is not yet settled.

FAQs

Questions parents ask us most

Straight answers. Anything else, just ask a counsellor.

Is an MD from Georgia valid in India?
It can be, if the course meets every NMC FMGL 2021 condition: at least 54 months of study, taught in English, a 12-month internship at the same university, and a licence to practise in Georgia on par with Georgian graduates. Georgia’s six-year MD covers the first three. The licence point is under discussion in 2026, so we show you the latest NMC position in writing before you pay. You then clear FMGE or NExT to practise in India.
Can I still join a state university?
Not as a new student. On 5 December 2025 Georgia’s Education Minister announced that state universities will stop admitting new foreign students from the next academic year, apart from exchange and bilateral programmes. Students already enrolled continue as before. New international students now join private universities, which remain open to them. See the 2026 changes above.
What is the “junior doctor” question about?
Georgian MD graduates can work as junior doctors, under supervision; independent practice needs a state exam and residency. On 24 September 2026 NMC said junior doctor status meets its licence rule only if it legally allows practice at the required level. Georgia’s accreditation body, NCEQE, replied on 14 October 2026 that foreign and Georgian graduates receive identical rights and a registration number. We track this closely and brief every family before admission.
How long is the course?
Six years, worth 360 ECTS credits. The course is called MD (Doctor of Medicine). Clinical rotations start in the fourth year and the sixth year is a full internship in the university’s clinics and teaching hospitals.
Is it taught in English?
Yes. In English-medium MD programmes the entire course, including clinical training, is taught in English, according to Georgia’s accreditation body NCEQE. Universities also teach basic Georgian so that you can talk to patients.
How much does a year cost?
An average year costs about ₹9.8 lakh all-in: roughly ₹5.7 lakh tuition, ₹3.2 lakh hostel and meals, and about ₹85,000 for personal costs, visa, residence permit and insurance (indicative, Oct 2026). Tuition at most private universities is US$5,500–7,000 a year, so six years of tuition typically comes to ₹31–40 lakh. See the fee breakdown.
Do I need NEET?
Yes. NMC requires every Indian student who studies medicine abroad to qualify NEET-UG, and the result stays valid for three years. The Georgian study-visa checklist in India also asks for your NEET scorecard.
How far is Georgia from India?
Chennai to Tbilisi takes about 9 hours with one stop, usually in Abu Dhabi or Sharjah. There is no direct flight. Georgia is one and a half hours behind Indian time.
What is the weather like?
Four seasons. Tbilisi summers reach about 30°C in July; January days are around 5°C and nights just below freezing. Spring and autumn are mild, with highs around 18°C.
