Chapter 05

The NMC rulebook (FMGL 2021)

54 moMinimum course
12 moInternship, same institution
EnglishFor the whole course
+12 moCRMI in India after FMGE

Your degree must also have

  • Indian MBBS subjects and real, in-person clinical training.
  • Eligibility for a licence in that country, on par with its graduates.
  • The same institution and country throughout — no offshore “India campus”.

NMC notice, 18 Mar 2026: online years must be made up, and the internship must finish within ten years of joining abroad. NMC publishes no “approved list” — it checks each graduate’s course.

Chapter 07

Myth vs. fact

Myth

Every foreign degree is valid in India.

Fact

Only degrees that meet FMGL 2021, then FMGE and CRMI.

Myth

An NMC advisory means the country is banned.

Fact

Advisories name concerns. Uzbekistan’s 2026 advisory is precautionary.

Myth

English medium means no local language.

Fact

Patients speak it. You need it on the wards.

Myth

Internship “done in India” is fine.

Fact

It must be at the same foreign institution.

Myth

“100% FMGE pass rate.”

Fact

No official university-wise data exists. Ask for the source.

Myth

NExT is coming, so skip FMGE prep.

Fact

NExT is years away. FMGE stays.

Chapter 10

8 questions for any university

  1. Is it recognised in its own country?
  2. Is every year taught in English?
  3. Where is the 12-month internship?
  4. Can graduates get a licence there?
  5. Is it named in any NMC or embassy advisory?
  6. What is the total cost for the whole course?
  7. What hostel and safety support exists?
  8. Can I speak to current Indian students?

Red flags — walk away

  • Internship “done in India” or not shown in writing.
  • Fees into a personal account, or “seat only today”.
  • “Guaranteed registration” or “100% FMGE pass”.

Chapter 12

Parents’ corner

Money

Budget the whole course plus FMGE coaching. Nepal and Bangladesh collect large sums up front; Indian cards don’t work in Russia.

Safety

Hostels with 24-hour security, local registration within days, and embassy numbers saved on day one.

Documents to keep ready

  • Passport
  • NEET-UG scorecard
  • Class 10 & 12 mark sheets
  • Birth certificate
  • Photos & medical fitness
  • Bank statements

Choosing the right university matters far more than choosing the lowest fee.
