Chapter 01

Your roadmap after Class 12

MBBS abroad is a 7-to-8-year path with clear checkpoints. Here is the whole road.

  1. Class 12 with PCB + English INDIAAge 17 by 31 December of the admission year.
  2. Qualify NEET-UG INDIAMandatory to practise in India, even with a foreign degree.
  3. Try Indian counselling first INDIAAbroad is a parallel plan, not a replacement.
  4. Choose an NMC-compliant university ABROADCheck course length, English medium, internship and licence.
  5. Study 54+ months, then a 12-month internship ABROADSame institution, same country, in person.
  6. Pass FMGE (NExT when it arrives) INDIAThe NBEMS screening test for foreign graduates.
  7. 12-month CRMI internship in India INDIAThen permanent registration — you are a doctor in India.

Chapter 04

The NMC rulebook (FMGL 2021)

54 moMinimum course
12 moInternship, same institution
100%English-medium teaching
+12 moCRMI in India after FMGE

Must-haves

  • Curriculum equal to Indian MBBS, with real hospital training.
  • Recognised institution, eligible for a local licence at par with citizens.
  • No offshore or “partner” study centres outside the country.

New in 2026 (NMC, 18 Mar 2026): the internship must finish within ten years of joining the foreign institution. Verify on nmc.org.in before paying.

Chapter 06

Myth vs. fact

Myth

MBBS abroad is only for low NEET scores.

Fact

Many high scorers choose it for affordability, exposure or a USMLE pathway.

Myth

Every foreign degree is valid in India.

Fact

Only degrees that meet FMGL 2021, followed by FMGE and CRMI.

Myth

The cheapest university is the smartest choice.

Fact

A ₹2–3 lakh saving means little if clinical exposure is weak.

Myth

“This university has a 100% FMGE pass rate.”

Fact

NBEMS publishes no university-wise rates. Ask for the source.

Chapter 09

10 questions for any university

  1. Is it recognised in its own country?
  2. Does it meet the FMGL Regulations?
  3. Is every year taught in English?
  4. Where — and how much — clinical exposure?
  5. Where is the 12-month internship done?
  6. What is the total cost beyond tuition?
  7. Is it named in any NMC / embassy advisory?
  8. What hostel and safety support exists?
  9. Can I speak to current Indian students?
  10. Have I verified all this in writing?

Red flags

  • Internship structure not shown in writing.
  • Fees requested in a personal account.
  • “Guaranteed registration” or “100% FMGE pass”.

Chapter 12

Life abroad & costly mistakes

Stay & food

Furnished hostels with Wi-Fi and security. Indian food is easy to find where the community is large.

SIM & banking

Local SIM on arrival, a forex or international debit card, and a budgeting app.

Costly mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing only because it is cheap.
  • Ignoring FMGL rules and NMC / embassy advisories.
  • Not asking about hidden costs.
  • Starting FMGE prep after graduation.

Choosing the right university matters far more than choosing the lowest fee.
