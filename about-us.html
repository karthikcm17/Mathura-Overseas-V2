Our story

Built on one simple idea — do right by the student

Mathura Overseas started because too many capable students were losing a real shot at a medical career simply because an Indian government seat wasn’t within reach that year — and too many “abroad MBBS” options were vague on fees, vague on recognition, and nowhere to be found once the first instalment was paid.

So we built our practice the other way around: we only work with universities we can vouch for, we quote the full fee structure upfront, and our team — based right here in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu — stays reachable throughout the years a student is actually abroad.

That approach has taken students from a NEET scorecard to a medical degree abroad, and many are now practising doctors — some back in India after completing their licensing requirements, others continuing their careers overseas.

Our mission

Every student deserves an honest shot at medicine

A NEET score that falls short of a government seat isn’t the end of a medical career — it’s a fork in the road. Our mission is to make sure that fork leads somewhere real: a recognised degree, a fair fee, and a support system that’s still there in year four, not just at admission.
