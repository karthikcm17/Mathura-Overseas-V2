How we choose

Why a university makes this list

Being popular is not enough. These are the four checks every university passes before we recommend it to your family.

Built to NMC’s rules

At least 54 months of study, English throughout and a 12-month internship at the same university, as FMGL 2021 requires.

Official lists, not reputation

We follow each country’s regulator. In Kyrgyzstan, for example, we list only universities with full 2026 state accreditation or state status.

English for every year

Some courses switch language halfway. Where that risk exists, as in Russia, the university confirms English for all six years in writing.

Fees in writing, paid directly

You get every fee on paper before you commit, and you pay the university directly, against an official receipt.
