01

Who we are

Mathura Overseas (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an MBBS-abroad admissions consultancy based in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. We guide students and their families through choosing a university abroad, applying, getting a visa, travelling, and settling in.

This policy covers personal data we receive through mathuraoverseas.com, phone calls, WhatsApp, email, social media messages and in-person counselling at our office. For the data you share with us, we are the Data Fiduciary — the business that decides why and how your data is used — and you are the Data Principal.

The laws this policy follows

The Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, which apply today; and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 with the DPDP Rules, 2025. As of October 2026, most DPDP duties take effect in May 2027 — we have chosen to follow those standards already.

Sensitive information

Some admission documents count as sensitive personal data under Indian law — for example a medical fitness certificate (health information) or bank and education-loan papers (financial information). We collect these only when a university, embassy or lender requires them, only with your consent, and we share them only with that organisation.

  • We never ask for card numbers, CVVs, UPI PINs, OTPs or net-banking passwords — by phone, WhatsApp or any other way. If someone claiming to be us asks, please do not share them and tell us straight away.
03

How we use your data

We use your personal data only for the purpose you shared it for, or a closely related one you would reasonably expect:

  • Counselling — calling you back, checking eligibility and suggesting universities that match your score, budget and preferences.
  • Admission — preparing and submitting applications and following up with universities.
  • Visa and travel — helping with visa files, tickets, forex and pre-departure briefings.
  • Support abroad — staying in touch with you and your parents until you graduate.
  • Updates you asked for — NEET and counselling news, admission deadlines. You can stop these at any time (see section 11).
  • Running the business safely — keeping records, preventing spam and fraud, and meeting legal and tax obligations.

Our legal basis

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form or share documents, or because you voluntarily provided it for a specific purpose (such as asking us to call you). Where a law requires us to keep or disclose data, we do so on that basis. We do not use your data for automated decisions about you.

05

Data sent outside India

Studying abroad means some of your data must travel abroad: your application goes to universities and embassies in your destination country. Our service providers may also store data on servers outside India.

We send data abroad only for the purposes in this policy, and never to a country the Government of India has restricted for such transfers under the DPDP Act.

07

How we protect it

We use reasonable security practices suited to a consultancy of our size:

  • This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Enquiry records sit in a password-protected business account with two-step verification, open only to our counselling team.
  • Paper documents are kept securely at our office and returned or destroyed when no longer needed.
  • Team members may access only the student files they are working on, and are bound to keep them confidential.

If something goes wrong

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of your personal data happens, we will tell you without delay — what happened, the likely impact, what we are doing, and what you can do — and we will inform the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

08

Your rights

Whether you are a student or a parent acting for one, you can ask us to:

  • See your data

    A summary of the data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we have shared it with.

  • Correct or update it

    Fix anything inaccurate, complete what is missing, or update what has changed.

  • Delete it

    Erase data we no longer need, unless a law requires us to keep it.

  • Withdraw consent

    As easily as you gave it. This stops future use; it does not undo what was lawfully done before.

  • Nominate someone

    Name a person who can use these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

  • Raise a complaint

    Tell our Grievance Officer if you are unhappy with how we handled your data.

How to make a request

  1. Contact us by email, WhatsApp or phone using the details in section 14, saying what you would like us to do.
  2. We confirm it’s you, usually by checking the mobile number you registered with, so nobody else can access your data.
  3. We act within 30 days and tell you what we did. If we cannot do something, we explain why.

Making a request is free. Please give accurate information and do not make requests on behalf of someone else without their authority — the DPDP Act places these duties on Data Principals too.

09

Students under 18

Indian law treats anyone under 18 as a child, and many NEET aspirants are 17 when they first contact us. So:

  • We ask that a parent or legal guardian fills in our forms, or is present, when a student is under 18.
  • Before we process a minor’s documents, we obtain the consent of a parent or guardian, and we may check that they are an adult.
  • We never track the behaviour of minors or show them targeted advertising.
  • If we learn that a student under 18 contacted us without a parent’s involvement, we will reach the parent or delete the details.
10

Cookies and browser storage

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or tracking pixels.

It does use your browser’s local storage for one practical job: if you submit a form while your phone is offline, a copy waits on your device and is sent automatically when you are back online, then removed. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

If we ever add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this section first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

11

Calls and messages

When you share your number, you agree that our counsellors may call you, and message you on WhatsApp or SMS, about MBBS admissions — even if your number is on a Do-Not-Disturb list, since you asked us to contact you.

Not interested any more? Tell the counsellor, reply “STOP” on WhatsApp, or email us. We will stop promotional messages within a few days, though we may still contact current students about their admission.

12

Other websites

Our pages link to university websites, government portals and our Facebook, Instagram and YouTube pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your data. Please read their policies before sharing information with them.
