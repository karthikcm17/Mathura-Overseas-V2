# Mathura Overseas — Image Brief (Home page)

Every photo sits in an **image slot**. Until a file exists, the slot shows a dashed box with the exact file name and size. Save the file with that exact name in that exact folder and it appears automatically — no code changes.

The home hero has **no photo** any more — that space is our eligibility / cost / compare tool.

## Rules for every image
- **Format:** WebP (quality ~80) for new images. Destination photos stay `.jpg` (other pages already use those names).
- **Weight:** under 150 KB each (under 200 KB for destination photos). Compress at squoosh.app.
- **No text, logos or watermarks inside the photo** (only the share image may have text).
- **Safe zone:** keep the main subject inside the **centre 70%** of the frame. Cards and phones crop the edges.
- **Tone:** natural light, slightly cool/blue-friendly colours so photos sit well on the navy-blue design.
- **People:** warm, confident, real-looking; Indian students/families wherever people appear.

## The idea for each image

**0. Handbook cover — `handbook-cover.webp` (600 × 800)**
A clean mock-up of the printed handbook: portrait cover in navy blue with the title "MBBS Abroad Handbook 2026–27", the Mathura Overseas logo and a small globe / flight-arc motif, slightly angled with a soft shadow on a plain light background (or transparent). It sits on a blue card, so leave a little margin around the book.

**1. Philippines — `philippines.jpg`**
Bright daytime city view: modern towers beside a historic stone church (Cebu/Manila feel), blue sky, some greenery. Modern, safe, international.

**2. Timor-Leste — `timor-leste.jpg`**
Dili waterfront: calm blue sea, palm trees, a clean promenade, a university-style building or the coastline in soft morning light. Tropical but quiet and welcoming.

**3. Uzbekistan — `uzbekistan.jpg`**
The blue-tiled historic madrasas of Registan, Samarkand, at golden hour, with a few students walking in the foreground for scale. Rich blue and gold.

**4. Vietnam — `vietnam.jpg`**
Hanoi at dusk: modern skyline over a lake, warm city lights, a few green trees. Energetic, close to India, modern.

**5. Trust banner — `counsellor-family.webp` (1600 × 900)**
Inside a friendly office: a counsellor (smart-casual) sitting with a mother, father and their teenage daughter, looking together at a brochure or laptop. Smiling, relaxed, natural window light. Should feel like "someone we can trust", not a stock handshake.
*Desktop:* shown full-width **behind** the headline, so put the people on the **right half** of the frame and keep the left half calm (wall, window, soft blur).
*Phone:* the same photo is shown **above** the text as a 4:3 crop that favours the right side. Keep **all faces between 30% and 95% of the width and around the vertical middle** so nobody is cut off on either layout.

**6. Student avatar — `priyadharshini-sekar.webp` (400 × 400)**
A **real photo** of Priyadharshini Sekar (only with her permission): face centred, shoulders up, plain light background, smiling. Shown as a small circle, so keep the face large.

**7. Share image — `home.jpg` (1200 × 630)**
A wide branded graphic: the "Mathura Overseas" logo, the line "Your Dream of Becoming a Doctor Has No Borders", and a student photo or the globe/flight-arc motif on navy blue. The only image allowed to contain text.
