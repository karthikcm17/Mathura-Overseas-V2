Why the Philippines

Six reasons Indian students choose the Philippines

Not brochure slogans — the things that actually shape your years as a medical student.

English, everywhere

English is an official language and the language of instruction. Lectures, textbooks, exams and patient conversations all happen in English — no year lost to learning a new language.

A US-pattern MD

Medical education follows the American model: a pre-medical degree, then a Doctor of Medicine built around clinical reasoning — the same way of thinking that licensing exams test worldwide.

Cases you will see at home

Tropical illnesses such as dengue, tuberculosis and typhoid are common in both countries, so the patients you treat on the wards look a lot like the ones waiting for you in India.

Confirmed in writing

In May 2025, the Philippines’ Commission on Higher Education (CHED) wrote to the Indian Embassy confirming that its MD meets India’s FMGL requirements.

Doors beyond India

According to CHED, graduates heading to the USA, UK, Canada or Australia follow those countries’ own exams, such as the USMLE — so the MD can be a first step to a global career.

Close to home

Only two and a half hours ahead of India, with one-stop flights from Chennai. Calls home fit easily into your day, and a large Indian student community means you won’t be the first from your town.

How the course works

From Class 12 to doctor: the Philippine route

The Philippines has no direct MBBS. You complete a short pre-medical degree, then the MD. Here is how the years add up.

  1. 1
    About 1.5–2 years

    Pre-medical degree (BS)

    A science-focused degree that prepares you for the MD. You sit the NMAT entrance test before moving on.

    Not counted by NMC
  2. 2
    54 months

    Doctor of Medicine (MD)

    Basic medical sciences first, then clinical clerkship in the university’s affiliated hospitals. This is the part NMC counts as your medical degree.

  3. 3
    12 months

    Internship

    At the same university or its attached hospital — an NMC requirement.

  4. 4
    Back in India

    FMGE / NExT

    Clear the licensing exam, complete any internship NMC asks of you, and register to practise.

In all, plan for roughly 7 years after Class 12. Your counsellor will confirm the exact plan for your university.

NMC status · as of Oct 2026

What’s confirmed — and what’s still open

We would rather you hear this from us today than discover it in your final year. Here is exactly where things stand for Indian students.

Confirmed

  • CHED letter, 8 May 2025: an MD from a CHED-recognised school meets India’s FMGL requirements.
  • The conditions: a 54-month MD, NMC Schedule-I subjects, English throughout, and a 12-month internship at the same institution.
  • House Resolution No. 73, 11 March 2026: the Philippine House endorsed the MD as compliant with NMC rules for Indian students.
Still open

The Philippine licence exam for Indian graduates

Foreign graduates may sit the Philippine licence exam (PLE) only if their home country offers Filipinos the same rights. The Embassy of India’s advisory of 17 June 2026 says guidance from Indian authorities on this is still awaited. It matters because NMC’s rules expect you to be eligible for a licence in the country where you studied.

Our promise: before you pay anything, we show you the latest Embassy and NMC advisories in writing — and we only recommend CHED-recognised schools that meet every condition above.

Source: Embassy of India, Manila — Advisory on Medical Education in the Philippines (17 June 2026). Rules can change; we re-check them for every student.

Eligibility

Can you apply? The NMC checklist

Every point below must be true for your degree to count in India.

  • NEET-UG qualifiedRequired for every Indian student studying medicine abroad. The result stays valid for three years.
  • 50% in Physics, Chemistry and BiologyIn Class 12, with English as a subject. 40% for SC, ST and OBC candidates.
  • 17 years old by 31 DecemberOf the year you join.
  • NMAT before the MDTaken during the pre-medical course; the score should be no more than two years old when the MD begins.
  • A course that meets FMGL 2021An MD of at least 54 months, taught in English, with a 12-month internship at the same university.
  • A CHED-recognised medical schoolWe verify this for every university before we recommend it.

FAQs

Questions parents ask us most

Straight answers. Anything else, just ask a counsellor.

Is an MD from the Philippines valid in India?
It can be, if the school is CHED-recognised and the course meets every NMC FMGL condition: a 54-month MD, English medium, NMC Schedule-I subjects and a 12-month internship at the same institution. CHED confirmed this in a letter dated 8 May 2025. You then clear FMGE or NExT to practise in India. One point is still open as of October 2026 — see NMC status above.
Does NMC count the pre-medical BS course?
No. NMC counts only the Doctor of Medicine (MD). The pre-medical BS is a stepping stone into the MD, so plan for its time and fees separately.
How long does it take in total?
About 1.5–2 years of pre-medical study, a 54-month MD and a 12-month internship — roughly 7 years after Class 12.
What is the NMAT?
The National Medical Admission Test, required to enter a Philippine MD programme. Students usually take it during the pre-medical course, and the score should be no more than two years old when the MD begins.
How much does a year cost?
An average year costs about ₹8 lakh all-in: roughly ₹3.8 lakh tuition, ₹2.8 lakh hostel and meals and ₹1.3 lakh personal costs (indicative, Sep 2026). Tuition for the full course typically ranges from ₹18 to 35 lakh. See the fee breakdown.
Do I need NEET?
Yes. NMC requires every Indian student who studies medicine abroad to qualify NEET-UG, and the result stays valid for three years.
Can I work in the USA after an MD from the Philippines?
According to CHED, graduates who plan to practise in the USA, UK, Canada or Australia do not need the Philippine licence exam, provided they clear those countries’ own exams and requirements, such as the USMLE.
Is the Philippines safe for Indian students?
Cities such as Cebu, Davao and Dagupan have hosted Indian medical students for years. As anywhere, follow hostel rules, keep your ACR I-Card and passport safe, and stay in touch with the Embassy of India in Manila. Our team stays in contact throughout the course.
