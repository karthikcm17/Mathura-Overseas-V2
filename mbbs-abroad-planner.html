    Ask your question
    How long is my NEET score valid for MBBS abroad?

    For the year you passed NEET plus the following two years. Admission to MBBS in India uses only the current year’s score — studying abroad gives you more time.

    Why is the cutoff lower for some categories?

    The qualifying score is lower for OBC, SC and ST candidates. For NEET 2026 it is 213 for General and 177 for OBC / SC / ST.

    What Class 12 marks do I need?

    At least 50% in Physics, Chemistry and Biology together for General, and 40% for OBC / SC / ST.

    Is there a minimum age?

    Yes — you must be at least 17 on 31 December of the year you take admission.

    Do the fees include hostel and food?

    Tuition covers tuition only. The cost planner adds average living costs for the whole course; flights, visa and insurance are extra.

    Can I do my internship in India instead?

    No. NMC requires a 12-month internship at the same foreign university. After you return, you also complete a 12-month supervised internship in India.

    How accurate are these figures?

    They are averages from our MBBS Abroad Handbook 2026–27 and published 2026 fee guides, as of Oct 2026. Fees differ by university and intake, so a counsellor confirms the exact figure before you decide.
